## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a premium editorial viewpoint engineered for mass-market retail scalability. It pairs the restraint, spacious typesetting, and architectural discipline of high-fashion broadsheets with the ruthless conversion clarity required of high-volume digital commerce.

### Brand Personality & Core Tenets
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium appeal without ornamental excess. Content is prioritized through structured negative space, deliberate borders, and typographic scale rather than decorative gimmickry.
- **Pragmatic Utility:** Mass-market operations demand friction-free sizing, legible currency representations (₹ INR), rapid inventory discovery, and instantaneous feedback.
- **Architectural Precision:** Every container, dividing line, and surface adheres to a strict linear logic. Forms are deliberately squared off with tight, disciplined micro-radii (2px–4px).

### Design Style Movement
- **Editorial Minimalism:** Defined by off-white parchment grounds, stark ink-black typography, crisp hairlines, and strict structural alignment. Gradients and ambient blurs are excluded in favor of tangible flat surfaces, disciplined linework, and high contrast.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires confidence, discernment, and trust. The visual tone mirrors an archival lookbook: considered, curated, and enduring.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict light mode to echo tactile editorial print. It maintains WCAG AA contrast across all text and functional interactive elements (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy; 3:1 for large display headlines and graphical inputs).

### Color Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#FBFBFA` (warm editorial white).
  - Surface Elevation & Cards: `#F4F3F0` (subtle grounding neutral).
- **Ink & Typography:** 
  - Primary Content / High Contrast Elements: `#141413` (near-black, yielding >16:1 contrast ratio against the base canvas).
  - Subdued / Secondary Metadata: `#595855` (neutral slate, passing WCAG AA for body text at 4.6:1+).
- **Structural Dividers & Outlines:**
  - Hairlines: `#E5E3DC` (light border for card shells, table rules, and non-interactive dividers).
  - Heavy Focus & Control Borders: `#D6D3CA` (interactive controls and structural borders).
- **Editorial Accents (Used Sparingly):**
  - Accent Primary: `#5A1827` (deep oxblood, reserved for high-intent alerts, badges, and curated collection callouts).
  - Accent Deep: `#4A121A` (pressed state, dark badge fills).
  - Status Success: `#1B3B2B` (deep forest green for stock confirmations, order success, and promotional markers).
  - Status Alert: `#7C1A22` (functional error messaging, 4.8:1 contrast).

Gradients are strictly prohibited as primary visual devices or button fills. All surfaces remain flat and tactile.

## Typography

Typography governs the brand experience. Playfair Display conveys editorial poise in catalog hero displays, lookbook sections, and primary headlines. Inter anchors the transactional and functional experience—ensuring legibility on dense product listing pages (PLPs), cart checkout funnels, and filter drawers.

### Typographic Rules
- **Punctuation & Syntax:** Em dashes (—) are strictly prohibited across all interfaces and marketing copy. Use hyphens, commas, colons, or clean line breaks.
- **Price Formatting:** Prices must always be formatted in Indian Rupee format (`₹`, U+20B9) using `Inter` with tabular figures enabled. Proper comma segmentation is required (e.g., `₹1,499`, `₹12,990`, `₹1,24,500`).
- **Uppercase Labels:** Functional metadata, badges, table headers, and category tags utilize `label-md` or `label-sm` set to uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.05em` to `0.06em`) for clear scans.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 8px layout grid governs all structural spacing, offsets, and component architecture, using 4px (`space-xs`) strictly for micro-alignments, tags, and inline badges.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and wider):** 12-column fluid grid. Outer margins are fixed to `margin` (48px / 3rem), with `gutter` (24px / 1.5rem) column spacing. Maximum content container width is capped at 1440px for editorial catalog stability.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid. Outer margins scale to 32px (2rem), gutters maintain 24px (1.5rem).
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outer margins scale to `margin-mobile` (16px / 1rem), with `gutter-mobile` (16px / 1rem). Product grids transition smoothly to 2-column masonry or 2-column square tiles.

### Spacing Discipline
Layout rhythm prioritizes deliberate white space. Product listing tiles, editorial narrative blocks, and transactional panels maintain vertical separation using `space-2xl` (48px) and `space-3xl` (64px) on desktop to establish an unhurried, luxury browsing cadence without losing operational efficiency.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through structural linework, subtle tonal shifts, and flat planar surfaces. Heavy dropped shadows, colorful glows, and frosted glass treatments are eliminated.

### Low-Contrast Outlines & Tonal Planes
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#FBFBFA` serves as the primary canvas for body regions and main grid spaces.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Card frames, utility panels, and modal containers use `#F4F3F0` backed by a 1px solid border in `#E5E3DC`.
- **Dividers & Structural Grid Borders:** Structural boundaries employ crisp `#E5E3DC` lines. 
- **Floating Overlays & Drawers:** High-elevation components (cart drawer, filter slideouts, search modal) rest on a 1px solid outline of `#D6D3CA`. A restrained ambient drop shadow is allowed exclusively for modal separation: `0 8px 30px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses tailored geometry. Large corner radiuses, pill shapes, and oval geometry are strictly prohibited.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Standard Controls & Elements (2px):** Checkboxes, radio indicators, color swatch frames, input fields, badges, and primary action buttons utilize a minimal radius of 2px (`border-radius: 2px`).
- **Cards & Elevated Containers (4px):** Product gallery cards, modular drawers, popover menus, and modal dialog containers carry a maximum radius of 4px (`border-radius: 4px`).
- **No Pill Radii:** Under no circumstances should buttons, pill tags, chips, or inputs exceed 4px in corner radius. Form controls must present disciplined rectangular silhouettes.

## Components

All interactive components must maintain crisp styling, direct feedback, and immediate usability. Icons must be monochrome, clean SVG line representations with a uniform 1.5px stroke width. Emojis are never permitted in UI copy or components.

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** 
  - Style: Solid fill `#141413`, text `#FBFBFA`, font `Inter` 14px uppercase (`label-lg`), letter-spacing `0.04em`.
  - Dimensions: Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px (`space-lg`), corner radius 2px.
  - States: Hover shifts background to `#2E2D2B` over 150ms. Active/Pressed scales background to `#000000`. Focus ring exhibits a 2px offset solid `#141413` border.
- **Secondary Button:**
  - Style: Transparent fill, 1px solid border `#141413`, text `#141413`, font `Inter` 14px uppercase (`label-lg`).
  - Dimensions: Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px, corner radius 2px.
  - States: Hover shifts fill to `#141413` and text to `#FBFBFA` over 150ms. Active fill shifts to `#2E2D2B`.
- **Tertiary / Editorial Accent Button:**
  - Style: Solid fill `#5A1827`, text `#FBFBFA`, corner radius 2px.
  - States: Hover shifts fill to `#4A121A` over 150ms.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:**
  - Style: Height 48px, fill `#FBFBFA`, 1px solid outline `#D6D3CA`, padding 12px 16px, text `#141413`, font `Inter` 15px (`body-md`), radius 2px.
  - Focus State: Border transitions to 1.5px solid `#141413` over 150ms with no glow or outer drop shadow.
  - Error State: Border transitions to 1.5px solid `#7C1A22` with accompanying 12px error text beneath.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Shape: 18px × 18px square with 2px radius for checkboxes; 18px × 18px square (or 18px circle with a solid square pip) for radios.
  - Outline: 1.5px solid `#141413` on `#FBFBFA`.
  - Checked: Solid `#141413` fill containing an internal white SVG checkmark.

### Chips & Product Filters
- **Specification:**
  - Style: Rectangular box, height 36px, padding 8px 16px, 1px solid `#E5E3DC`, fill `#F4F3F0`, font `Inter` 13px (`body-sm`), corner radius 2px.
  - Selected State: Border changes to `#141413`, fill switches to `#141413`, text shifts to `#FBFBFA`.

### Product Cards
- **Specification:**
  - Surface: Flat, border 1px solid `#E5E3DC`, background `#FBFBFA`, corner radius 4px.
  - Image Ratio: 3:4 or 4:5 vertical crop with zero inner padding.
  - Content Block: 16px padding (`space-md`). 
  - Details: Brand/Category in uppercase `label-sm` (`#595855`), Title in `title-md` (`#141413`), and Price formatted cleanly (`₹2,499`) in `price-md`.
  - Hover Interaction: Image shifts to alternate editorial angle; title subtly underline-renders or deepens; quick-add bar slides up with 2px radius.

### Badges & Tags
- **Editorial Badges ("New Season", "Curated", "Sold Out"):**
  - Fill `#F4F3F0`, 1px solid `#D6D3CA`, text `#141413`, font 11px uppercase (`label-sm`), padding 4px 8px, corner radius 2px.
  - Accent / Sale Badge: Fill `#5A1827`, text `#FBFBFA`, font 11px uppercase (`label-sm`), corner radius 2px.
